Rhetorical Synthesis — Advanced Quick Reference

Five goal types and what each demands from your answer

Goal 1

Present + Emphasize Impact

Introduce the subject AND highlight its specific, quantified, or most significant effect. Both must be present.

Goal 2

Compare or Contrast

Show the difference or similarity between two items using specific details from both. Vague comparisons are wrong.

Goal 3

Support a Claim with Evidence

State the claim AND include the specific evidence. Not just one.

Goal 4

Introduce with Context

Place the subject within a broader framework before focusing on the specific contribution.

Goal 5

Qualify or Complicate a Claim

Acknowledge a limitation, exception, or complication of a claim made in the notes.

Syra's Method

1
Read the goal carefully — it is always specific. The goal says exactly what to emphasize. The correct answer must do exactly this.
2
Eliminate answers that do the wrong job. Many wrong answers accurately describe the notes but accomplish a different goal.
3
Check that all required elements are present. For contrast: both items must appear. For support: both claim and evidence. Missing one = wrong.
Top Traps
Accurate but wrong goal
Missing one required element
Too much irrelevant detail
Vague comparison instead of specific
