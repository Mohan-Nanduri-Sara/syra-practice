Statistics — SAT Reference

Core rules you must know cold for the data & statistics unit

Key Facts

Meansum of values ÷ number of values
Medianmiddle value (average of two middles if even count)
Modethe most frequently occurring value
Rangemaximum − minimum
Weighted average(sum of each group's total) ÷ (combined count)
Interquartile range (IQR)Q3 − Q1
Adding a Constant

Shifts mean and median, NOT the spread

Adding the same number to every value shifts the mean, median, and mode by that amount — but the range and standard deviation stay exactly the same.

Multiplying by a Constant

Scales every measure, including spread

Multiplying every value by a constant k multiplies the mean, median, range, AND standard deviation by that same k.

Outliers

The mean moves, the median barely does

A single extreme value can drag the mean far from the "typical" value, but the median is resistant to outliers since it only depends on the middle position.

Weighted Average

Never just average the group averages

Combining two groups' means requires weighting by each group's SIZE: total combined sum ÷ total combined count — not a simple average of the two means.

Standard Deviation

Zero means no spread at all

If every value in a dataset is identical, the standard deviation is exactly 0 — there's no variability to measure.

Watch Out For
Averaging two group means directly instead of weighting by group size
Confusing the mean and the median in a dataset with an outlier
Assuming adding a constant changes the range (it doesn't — only multiplying does)
Forgetting to sort the data before finding the median
