Basic Math Facts — SAT Reference

Core rules you must know cold before moving to the next topic

Key Facts

Number nesting orderNatural → Whole → Integer → Rational → Real
PEDMASParentheses, Exponents, then ×÷ left-to-right, then +− left-to-right
Distance on number line| bigger position − smaller position |
HCF × LCM= the product of the two numbers
Negative exponentx⁻ⁿ = 1 / xⁿ
Scientific notation forma × 10ⁿ, where 1 ≤ a < 10
Classification

Boxes inside boxes

Every Natural number is also Whole, Integer, Rational, and Real. Classify from the smallest box that fits, but remember all the bigger boxes still apply.

PEDMAS

Not a strict ranking

Multiplication does NOT outrank division, and addition does NOT outrank subtraction. When you see both in the same step, work strictly left to right.

LCM vs HCF

Which one does the word problem want?

"Largest possible size" or "split into equal groups" → HCF. "When do cycles align" or "smallest number divisible by both" → LCM.

Even / Odd

Must-be-true, not just-happens-to-be-true

The SAT rarely asks about ONE number — it asks what must hold for every possible case. Use the even/odd rules instead of testing a single example.

Exponents

Even power ≠ one solution

x² = k has TWO solutions (±√k) unless the problem restricts x to positive values. y³ = k has only one real solution.

SAT Trap

Negative base vs. negative sign

−3² means −(3²) = −9. (−3)² means (−3)×(−3) = 9. The parentheses change everything.

Watch Out For
Treating × as always before ÷
Missing the negative root of x²=k
Confusing HCF and LCM in word problems
Answers numerically equal but not in proper scientific notation form
