Vieta's Shortcuts

Sum and product without solving

For ax²+bx+c=0: sum of roots = −b/a, product of roots = c/a. Answers many questions instantly, with no factoring or formula needed.

Discriminant Fast-Track

Know the nature of roots before solving

b²−4ac tells you how many real roots exist before you do any more work: positive means two, zero means one repeated, negative means none.

System — No vs Infinite Solutions

Compare slope AND intercept

Same slope + different intercept → no solution (parallel lines). Same slope + same intercept → infinitely many solutions (identical line).

Radical Equations

Squaring can create extraneous roots

Isolate the radical, square both sides, solve — then ALWAYS verify each solution in the original equation. Squaring can introduce a root that doesn't actually work.

Sum & Sum-of-Squares

Find the product without finding the roots

If you know the sum of two roots and the sum of their squares, the product is [(sum)² − (sum of squares)] / 2 — no need to solve for the roots themselves.

Watch Out For
Flipping only one sign during factoring instead of checking both
Misapplying a coefficient ratio in the wrong direction when solving for an unknown constant
Treating coefficients as if they were the roots themselves
Forgetting to check a radical-equation solution for extraneous roots
