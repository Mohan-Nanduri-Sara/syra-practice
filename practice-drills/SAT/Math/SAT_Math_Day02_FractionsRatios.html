Fractions, Ratios & Proportionality — SAT Reference

Core rules you must know cold before moving to the next topic

Key Facts

Fraction (part-to-whole)part ÷ total
Ratio part of the wholethat part ÷ (sum of ALL parts)
Direct variationy = kx (y/x constant)
Inverse variationy = k/x (xy constant)
Combining ratiosscale both so the shared term matches
Alligation sharecloser value gets the BIGGER share (cross-wise)
Fraction of Remaining

The whole shrinks every step

In a sequential problem, each step's "whole" is what's LEFT from the previous step — not the original total. Redraw the whole at every step.

Ratio Type

Part-to-part vs. part-to-whole

a:b as a fraction of EACH OTHER is a/b directly. As a fraction of the TOTAL, it's a/(a+b). These give completely different numbers.

Combining Ratios

Match the shared term first

To combine A:B and B:C into A:B:C, scale BOTH ratios so the shared term (B) is identical in both before reading off the combined ratio.

Direct vs Inverse

Read the exact wording

"Varies directly" / "directly proportional" → y=kx. "Varies inversely" / "inversely proportional" → y=k/x. Always solve for k from the given pair first — never assume.

Power Variation

Scaling isn't 1-to-1

If y ∝ x², doubling x QUADRUPLES y — not doubles it. If y ∝ 1/x², tripling x shrinks y to 1/9 of its value.

Alligation

Closer gets more, cross-wise

When mixing two values to hit a target, the ratio of amounts is the CROSSED distances — the component closer to the target gets the bigger share.

Watch Out For
Applying a fraction to the ORIGINAL total instead of what's left
Reading a part:part ratio as a fraction of the total (or vice versa)
Confusing direct and inverse variation from the wording
Inverting the alligation ratio (giving the farther value the bigger share)

Number Fundamentals
Simplify the ratio 18 : 24 to lowest terms.

Fraction Fundamentals
A class has 12 boys and 18 girls. What fraction of the class is boys?
3
Ratio Basics
If a : b = 3 : 5 and a = 12, what is b?
4
Direct Variation
y varies directly with x. If y = 15 when x = 3, what is y when x = 7?
5
Inverse Variation
y varies inversely with x. If y = 8 when x = 5, what is y when x = 10?
6
Fraction of Remaining
A tank is 3/4 full. If 1/3 of the water currently in the tank is used, what fraction of the tank's total capacity remains?
7
Fraction of Remaining
A library donated 2/7 of its books in January, then donated 1/5 of the remaining books in February. What fraction of the original books remain?
8
Ratio to Fraction
In a fruit basket, apples, oranges, and bananas are in the ratio 4 : 3 : 2. What fraction of the basket is oranges?
9
Combining Ratios
If A : B = 2 : 5 and B : C = 3 : 4, what is A : B : C?
10
Direct Variation (Power)
The distance d an object falls varies directly with the square of the time t. If d = 20 meters when t = 2 seconds, how far does it fall when t = 5 seconds?
11
Inverse Variation (Power)
The intensity I of light varies inversely with the square of the distance r from the source. If I = 36 when r = 2, what is I when r = 6?
12
Alligation
A shop mixes tea worth $18/kg with tea worth $30/kg to produce a blend worth $22/kg. What is the ratio of the amount of $18 tea to $30 tea used?
13
Ratio + Percent Change
The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 5 : 7. After the number of boys increases by 20% and the number of girls increases by 10%, the new total number of students is 274. What was the original number of boys?
14
Combining Ratios (Chained)
A : B = 3 : 4, B : C = 5 : 6, and C : D = 2 : 3. What is A : D in simplest form?
15
Alligation
A wholesaler blends rice costing $40/kg with rice costing $55/kg to create a blend costing $46/kg, needing 45 kg of blend total. How many kg of the $40 rice are needed?
16
Inverse Variation (Power)
If y is inversely proportional to x², and x is tripled, by what factor does y change?
17
Direct Variation (Power)
If y is directly proportional to the cube of x, and x is halved, by what factor does y change?
18
Fraction of Remaining
A charity spent 1/4 of its fund on food, then 1/3 of the remaining fund on shelter, then half of what was left after that on medicine. What fraction of the original fund remains?
19
Ratio + Proportionality
The ratio of the radius of circle A to circle B is 2 : 3. If the area of a circle varies directly with the square of its radius, what is the ratio of the area of circle A to circle B?
20
Comprehensive Review
A school has students in Grade 9 and Grade 10 in the ratio 3 : 5. Grade 9's average score is 72, and Grade 10's average score is 84. What is the average score of all students combined?
